01 / SCOPE

About PixelShop

PixelShop (com.lucky.pixelshop) is an Android photo editor provided by official.batrasindustries. This policy covers the offline edition of the app and this privacy-policy page.

The editor does not require an account or an API key. It has no developer-operated photo-upload service, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Editing, layers, masks and effects are processed on your device.

02 / LOCAL INFORMATION

What the app handles on your device

  • Files you select: photos, images, stickers, editable projects and colour LUT files are read to provide the editing tools you request.
  • Your edits: layers, original pixels retained for editable effects, masks, text, effect settings and canvas settings are used to display and save your work. An editable project can contain original or hidden image content, not just the flattened image you see.
  • Preferences and presets: saved colour presets and app preferences remain in the app’s local storage.
  • Recovery and temporary files: a local recovery project helps restore the last editing session. Temporary files can be used while saving or exporting; the app attempts to remove export staging files when the operation finishes.
  • Optional export metadata: Artist, Copyright and Description values you enter can be saved in projects and JPG exports. Original camera and GPS metadata are not intentionally copied into exported images by the editor.

These editing files and settings are not automatically sent to the developer. PixelShop does not use your photos to train AI models or offer a remote AI editing service in this edition.

03 / NETWORK & SHARING

When information can leave your device

The offline editor does not upload your photos or editing activity. It does not sell your photo data, use it for advertising, or send it to developer analytics services.

  • Document providers: Android’s file picker may show Google Drive or another cloud provider. If you select or save a file there, that provider may transfer or sync the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Opening the online policy: When you visit the public version of this page in your browser, GitHub Pages may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser information and request time to deliver and secure the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. No advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or tracking pixels are added to this page by us.
  • Contacting support: if you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Email providers process those communications under their own policies. Do not send photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your request.

Android, your device manufacturer, your browser and your app store may independently handle diagnostic or service information according to their policies. This is separate from a PixelShop analytics service.

04 / ACCESS

File access stays under your control

PixelShop uses Android’s system document picker to open selected files and choose save locations. This edition does not request broad all-files access, location, contacts, microphone or camera permissions. The app itself does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Android may retain access to a project location you chose so that later saves can write to that location. Opening a web page or composing a support email is handled by the external browser or email app you choose. The bundled policy can be read without an internet connection.

05 / YOUR CONTROL

Retention and deletion

The recovery copy is updated as you work and may remain after you close the app. Local presets and preferences remain until removed or app storage is cleared. There is no PixelShop cloud account or server-side photo library to delete.

  1. Remove internal app data: use Android Settings → Apps → PixelShop → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall the app. This removes the app’s recovery project, presets and settings. Save anything you want to keep first.
  2. Remove exported or saved files: delete exported images and saved .pixelshop projects using your file manager or the document provider where you saved them. Clearing app storage does not delete these independently saved files.
  3. Remove cloud-provider copies: manage backups, trash and synced copies in that provider. PixelShop cannot delete copies held by another service.

Android app backup is disabled in this edition, and app-file recovery data is excluded from the configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device manufacturers and independently selected file providers may have their own backup behaviour.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete information you sent to support. We cannot remotely access or erase files kept only on your device.

Before sharing an editable project, check its layers, retained originals and metadata. A PNG or JPG export is a flattened image; an editable project may contain more information.

06 / SECURITY

Protecting your work

Private app data is stored within Android’s app storage protections. Exported files use the destination you select and are subject to that destination’s access controls. The app does not add password protection or its own encryption to exported images or editable project files.

Use a device screen lock, keep Android updated and choose file-sharing destinations carefully. No device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

07 / CHILDREN

Children’s privacy

PixelShop does not request an age, create accounts or knowingly collect children’s personal information through the offline editor. If a child has sent personal information to our support address, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal, subject to applicable requirements.

08 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the date shown above. The in-app offline copy is updated with app releases. The public web version, when available, provides the latest published policy. Any future feature that changes data handling will need corresponding information and, where required, consent.
